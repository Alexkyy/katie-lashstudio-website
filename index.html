What We Offer

The Services

Every set is designed around your natural eye shape, lash health, and lifestyle — no cookie-cutter lashes, only what's made for you.

01 Classic Lashes One extension per natural lash for a clean, fluttery look. Available in Half, Natural, Glam, or Drama. From$120
02 Hybrid Lashes A blend of classic & volume fans for beautiful texture. Available in Natural, Fullness, or Drama for a customized look. From$140
03 Korean Lash Lift A semi-permanent lift that curls your natural lashes from the root — mascara-free beauty that lasts weeks. Tint add-on available. From$80
04 Refill Appointments Keep your set full and fresh. Refills require a prior service with us and are available within 4 weeks of that appointment, for Classic and Hybrid sets. From$90
05 Thicker Lash Add-on Upgrade any Classic set to a thicker, more dramatic lash for extra softness, fullness, and impact. Available on request. Add-on+$10
06 Lash Removal Safe, gentle removal of existing lash extensions using professional-grade remover. Perfect for a fresh start or between sets. Flat rate$30

Meet Your Artist

Hi, I'm Katie

I have over eight years of experience in the beauty industry — a certified lash artist based in Lynnwood, WA, passionate about helping every client feel their most beautiful and confident. Lashes should look natural, feel weightless, and last as long as possible.

I specialize in Classic, Hybrid, and Korean lash lifts, carefully tailoring each service to your unique eye shape, lash health, and daily lifestyle. My studio is a calm, welcoming space where you can relax and trust you're in skilled hands.

I use only premium lashes and professional-grade adhesives to ensure your comfort and the longevity of every set.
